# Data Notice

This repository contains derived, publication-facing tables and figures for the Paper 3 seed manuscript only.

Raw SPARC rotmod files, raw H I survey products, FITS cubes, downloaded webpages, API responses, and private exploratory notes are not redistributed here. The retained inputs are derived artifacts inherited from the Paper 1 and Paper 2 reproducibility packages plus small derived radial pilot summaries needed to reproduce the Paper 3 candidate triage.

The package should be read as a reproducible candidate-search audit, not as a complete external validation data release.
